Turn numbers into stories that move.

DataStory Studio turns a table of figures into an animated bar chart race, ready to upload to YouTube, Shorts, Reels or TikTok — made entirely on your device.

Download on the App Store No account. No sign-in. Your data never leaves your phone.

What it does

Give it a table — GDP by country, goals by team, followers by year — and it renders a smooth, race-to-the-top animation with a synthesised soundtrack, ready to share.

Bring your own data

Import a CSV or type a table by hand in the Create tab. Everything stays on your device.

Ready-made stories

A growing library of sourced datasets — economies, populations, sport, technology and more — updated straight from the web when you ask.

Built to be shared

Export in 1080p or 4K in the aspect ratio each platform wants. The music is original and carries no licence obligations.

Quizzes & timelines too

Beyond chart races, build animated quizzes and timeline stories from the same simple, on-device workflow.

Points & rewarded video ads

DataStory Studio is free. Exporting videos uses points: you start with a balance, each export spends some, and you can top up at any time by watching a short rewarded video ad — your choice, never forced. Ads are the only way the app makes money, and they only ever appear when you tap to watch one.

What the ads mean for your privacy. Rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob. Before any ad loads, the app asks for your choice under Apple's App Tracking Transparency and Google's consent prompt, and it honours it. Nothing you create — your data, your videos — is ever sent to an advertiser.

Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Private by design

  • No account and no sign-in — nothing to create, nothing to remember.
  • Your datasets, videos and music stay on your device. Delete the app and they are gone; we never held a copy.
  • The app reaches the network only to fetch story data when you ask, to check for updates, and to load a rewarded ad when you choose to watch one.
